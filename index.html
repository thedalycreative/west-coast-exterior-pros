About Us

At West Coast Exterior Pros, we provide professional exterior cleaning services across Perth for both residential and commercial properties. From high-pressure driveway cleaning and roof gutter maintenance to window and solar panel cleaning, our expert team delivers visible results that enhance property appearance and longevity.

With over a decade of experience, we’ve earned a reputation for reliable, detailed work and outstanding customer service. Locally owned and operated, we bring trusted expertise and care to every job—no matter the size.

We support a wide range of clients including homeowners, real estate agents, business owners, and property managers. Our commercial exterior cleaning solutions extend to schools, offices, shopfronts, government buildings, and car parks—ensuring each site remains clean, safe, and inviting.

Committed to safety and sustainability, our fully insured team uses eco-friendly products and surface-specific equipment to deliver powerful cleaning results without risk of damage.

West Coast Exterior Pros is more than a service provider—we’re your local partner in long-term property care. Let us help you protect your investment and elevate your property’s curb appeal the professional way.
